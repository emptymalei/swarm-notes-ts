# Bayesian Hierarchical Panel Vector Autoregressions

> *Auto-generated stub. Edit this file to add more details.*

A hierarchical modeling framework that uses global-counterpart-centered priors for country-specific panel VAR models to improve estimation and forecasting.


## Why It Matters

This framework enables effective parameter shrinkage and information sharing across heterogeneous units in panel time series.

## Evidence

> It includes a family of Bayesian hierarchical panel Vector Autoregressions (VARs) that are characterised by: (i) country-specific VAR models (ii) with their parameters' priors centred around their global counterparts
