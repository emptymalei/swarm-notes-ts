## Summary

This paper utilizes high-resolution, seeing-free stratospheric near-UV spectroscopy from the Sunrise-III/SUSI instrument to characterize oscillatory power across diverse magnetic environments in a solar active region. By performing a multi-line analysis of Doppler-velocity time series, the authors reveal that oscillatory power is not globally uniform but varies significantly according to the local magnetic field strength and the specific spectral line observed. This finding challenges traditional one- or two-line measurement approaches, arguing that multi-line diagnostics are essential for fully resolving the local wave spectrum.

## Key Contributions

- Demonstrates environment-dependent redistribution of oscillatory power (2-4 mHz vs 4-12 mHz) across weak-field, plage, sunspot, and pore regions.
- Establishes that oscillatory power measurement is significantly line-dependent even for lines at comparable formation depths, requiring multi-line diagnostic approaches.
- Provides high-resolution, seeing-free mapping of local wave spectra using near-UV spectroscopy from the Sunrise-III/SUSI mission.
