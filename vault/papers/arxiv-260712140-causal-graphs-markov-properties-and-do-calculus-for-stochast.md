# Causal Graphs, Markov Properties and Do-calculus for Stochastic Differential Equations

**Authors**: Philip Boeken, Joris M. Mooij
**Date**: 2026-07-13
**Paper ID**: [arxiv:2607.12140](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12140)

## Summary

This paper develops a formal causal framework for systems of stochastic differential equations (SDEs) by defining clear semantics for observational and interventional distributions. The authors establish fundamental graphical properties, such as σ-separation and a d-separation variant for additive-noise SDEs, which validate the use of do-calculus in continuous-time dynamical systems. By introducing time-split systems, the framework bridges the gap between path-level causality and discrete subsampled time-series, allowing for the direct application of established causal discovery algorithms like PC and FCI.

## Key Contributions

- Formulates a rigorous causal semantics for systems of stochastic differential equations (SDEs), establishing solvability conditions for valid observational and interventional distributions.
- Proves the σ-separation Markov property and extends the Pearl do-calculus to continuous-time SDE systems, providing a formal bridge between causal graphs and sample-path interventional effects.
- Introduces time-split systems to unify continuous-time causal reasoning with discrete-time analysis, enabling direct application of constraint-based causal discovery algorithms (PC, FCI) to SDE-based systems.
