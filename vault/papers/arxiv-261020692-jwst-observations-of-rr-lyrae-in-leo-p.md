# JWST Observations of RR Lyrae in Leo P

**Authors**: Catherine M. Slaughter, Alessandro Savino, Meredith Durbin, Andrew E. Dolphin, Kristen B. W. McQuinn, Max J. B. Newman, Evan D. Skillman, Daniel R. Weisz
**Date**: 2026-10-14
**Paper ID**: [arxiv:2610.20692](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20692)

## Summary

This paper presents a cross-telescope analysis of RR Lyrae variable stars in the low-metallicity dwarf galaxy Leo P using co-spatial time-series photometry from HST/ACS and JWST/NIRCam. By extracting periods, amplitudes, and mean magnitudes for the identified stars, the authors establish a robust distance estimate of 1.61 Mpc, consistent with prior measurements. Crucially, the study leverages the low-metallicity environment of Leo P to refine the Period-Wesenheit-Metallicity (PWZ) function for JWST filters, significantly increasing the precision of the calibration for future infrared-based distance scale studies.

## Key Contributions

- Identified and characterized 20 RR Lyrae stars in HST and 15 in JWST imaging within the low-metallicity dwarf galaxy Leo P.
- Determined a Gaia-consistent distance to Leo P of 1.61 +/- 0.06 Mpc using RR Lyrae-based PWZ functions, validating existing measurements.
- Improved the JWST-based PWZ calibration by combining Leo P and WLM data, reducing mean fractional uncertainty by 31% on the slope and 11% on the intercept.

## Limitations

The study relies on a relatively small sample size of RR Lyrae stars (15-20) in a single galaxy for the primary analysis.
