# Transverse impulse response of finite and infinite Rayleigh beams

**Authors**: Saranya Biswas, Anindya Chatterjee
**Date**: 2026-10-14
**Paper ID**: [arxiv:2610.19986](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19986)

## Summary

This paper presents a theoretical analysis of the transverse impulse response in both finite and infinite Rayleigh beams. For finite beams, the authors use modal expansion to derive a short-time asymptotic series for the response at the point of impact. For infinite beams, they obtain an exact solution using spectral integrals expressed through modified Bessel functions, demonstrating consistency between asymptotic results and Euler-Bernoulli beam theory. The study highlights physical phenomena such as high-frequency precursors and slope discontinuities that arise due to the finite length and group velocity characteristics of the beam.

## Key Contributions

- Derived a short-time asymptotic series for the midpoint impulse response of a finite, simply supported Rayleigh beam using modal expansion.
- Obtained an exact closed-form solution for the infinite beam impulse response via spectral integrals evaluated using modified Bessel functions.
- Characterized the transition between finite and infinite beam responses, specifically identifying high-frequency precursors and slope discontinuities related to group velocity limits.

## Limitations

The study is restricted to undamped beams and concentrated impulses, leaving damping effects and complex impact scenarios for future investigation.
