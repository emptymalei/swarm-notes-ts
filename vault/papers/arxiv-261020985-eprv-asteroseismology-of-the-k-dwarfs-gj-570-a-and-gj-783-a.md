# EPRV Asteroseismology of the K dwarfs GJ 570 A and GJ 783 A with ESPRESSO. Empirical mode-amplitude scaling in tension with 3D convection simulations

**Authors**: T. L. Campante, Y. Zhou, Y. Li, H. Kjeldsen, R. R. Costa, A. W. Neitzel, J. H. C. Martins, A. M. Silva, V. Adibekyan, J. Gomes da Silva, J. R. Larsen, S. G. Sousa, T. R. Bedding, D. **Date**: 2026-10-14
**Paper ID**: [arxiv:2610.20985](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20985)

## Summary

This study utilizes the ESPRESSO spectrograph to conduct high-precision radial-velocity asteroseismology on two K-type stars, GJ 570 A and GJ 783 A. By extracting low-amplitude solar-like oscillations from high-cadence time series, the authors reveal an empirical mode-amplitude scaling of approximately (L/M)^1.5 for mid-K dwarfs. This finding contrasts sharply with 3D radiative-hydrodynamical simulations, which predict a shallower (L/M)^0.9 scaling, exposing a significant discrepancy in our theoretical understanding of convection-driven oscillations in cool stars.

## Key Contributions

- Detection of solar-like oscillations in the mid-K dwarfs GJ 570 A and GJ 783 A using ESPRESSO, reaching precision levels of ~2.5-5 cm/s.
- Identification of a regime-specific scaling transition where mode amplitudes scale as ~(L/M)^1.5 for K dwarfs, significantly steeper than the ~(L/M)^0.7 observed in G dwarfs.
- Demonstration of a significant tension between observed mode amplitudes and first-principles 3D radiative-hydrodynamical simulations, which predict a much shallower ~(L/M)^0.9 scaling.

## Limitations

The study is limited to two target stars, and the discrepancy in scaling suggests missing physics in current convection-pulsation coupling models for cooler stellar atmospheres.
